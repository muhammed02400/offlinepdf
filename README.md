# OfflinePDF

Privacy policy for the **OfflinePDF** Android app.

OfflinePDF converts Office documents (`.pptx`, `.ppt`, `.docx`, `.doc`, `.xlsx`, `.xls`)
into PDF entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
